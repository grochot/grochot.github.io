<p style="text-align:justify;">I am assistant Professor at the Institute of Electronics, Department of Computer Science, Electronics and Telecommunications, AGH University of Krakow.
I am a graduate of the <a href="http://https://smp.uj.edu.pl/informacje-ogolne"> Interdisciplinary Mathematical and Natural Science Studies </a> at the Faculty of Physics, Astronomy and Applied Computer Science, Jagiellonian University, and the <a href="https://www.gov.pl/web/ncbr/interdyscyplinarne-srodowiskowe-studia-doktoranckie-fizyczne-chemiczne-i-biofizyczne-podstawy-nowoczesnych-technologii-i-inzynierii-materialowej--fcb--dr-krzysztof-grochot"> Interdisciplinary Environmental Doctoral Studies "Physical, Chemical and Biophysical Foundations of Modern Technologies and Materials Engineering" (FCB) </a> at the Faculty of Physics and Applied Computer Science, AGH, where I defended my PhD thesis in 2023.</p>

<p style="text-align:justify;">Professionally, I am associated with the <a href="http://maglay.agh.edu.pl"> Magnetic Multilayer Systems and Spin Electronics Group </a>, where I study thin-film magnetic systems by electrical methods, lithogaphics of spintronic nanodevices, and programming and data analysis. </p>

<p style="text-align:justify;">In my free time, I am engaged in activities popularizing natural sciences, including the organization of the <a href="http://swietlik.edu.pl"> Natural Science Competition 'Świetlik' </a>. </p>

<p style="text-align:justify;">As part of my teaching activities, I teach classes and seminars on web application programming, basics of Python programming and data science. </p>

<p style="text-align:justify;">I am interested in programming, technological innovations and economics.</p>
